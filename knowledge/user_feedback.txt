- QUESTION QUANTITY & RATIO (>= 5 QUESTIONS PER PAGE MANDATORY):
  - Every page MUST contain AT LEAST 5 distinct questions.
  - 80% of questions MUST be Content-Based (Visual Observation, Story Plot Comprehension, Character Feelings directly on Page N).
  - 20% of questions MUST be Elaboration / Open-Ended (Personal Connection, Real-World Knowledge).

- LEVEL-APPROPRIATE VOCABULARY FOR 10YO CHINESE ESL LEARNERS (A1/A2 LEVEL):
  - Questions and answers MUST use simple, everyday English.
  - AVOID overly complex/academic terms like "produce section", "department", or "retail establishment".
  - Use simple words instead (e.g. "fruit and vegetables" instead of "produce section").

- DIRECT & NATURAL COVER QUESTIONS:
  - Ask cover questions in the most direct, natural way.
  - USE: "What's the title of the book?"
  - AVOID: "What title do you see on the book cover?" or "What main title text is displayed?"

- CLEAN RETELLING GRAMMAR & PUNCTUATION:
  - Ensure the blanked words grammatically fit the sentence skeleton and parenthetical answers.
  - NEVER output trailing semicolons (;) or mismatched words in retelling lines.

- CONDITIONAL SCENARIO ELABORATION ("What do you need...?"):
  - Apply "What do you need when..." ONLY when the page depicts a significant scenario (swimming, riding a bike, cooking). Do NOT force it on minor pages.

- NO "MAKE A SENTENCE" QUESTIONS ON PAGES.
- NO childish role-play, TPR, or physical movement games (~10+ yo students).
- NO LETTER-COUNTING OR REPETITIVE VOCABULARY MEANINGS.
- Story questions must strictly match the original story tense (Past Tense for past stories, Present for present).
- Page N questions must ONLY ask about Page N. No "Looking back at page X" questions.

- INTERACTIVE VOCABULARY FORMAT (BLUE FONT):
  - Vocabulary entries MUST follow this exact pattern:
    word — Guiding/Interactive explanation! Simple interactive question?
  - Example: "moon — The big round white ball glowing in the night sky! Is the moon square or round?"
  - Example: "beach — Soft sand next to the big blue ocean! Have you been to a beach?"
  - NEVER repeat the word twice (AVOID "moon — moon — white ball").
  - NEVER use letter-counting gimmicks (AVOID "m-o-o-n, 4 letters").

- NO DUPLICATE VOCABULARY WORDS IN JSON:
  - In the JSON output, the "meaning" field MUST NOT repeat the "word" or include a dash.
  - BAD JSON: "word": "classroom", "meaning": "classroom — A special room..."
  - GOOD JSON: "word": "classroom", "meaning": "A special room with desks and chairs where students learn! What color is your classroom door?"

- GAME DIVERSITY MANDATE:
  - Do NOT default to "Magic Cover" or "Sentence Builder" for every book!
  - You MUST dynamically select 2 DIFFERENT game styles from the QKids Game Bank (Games A through O) based on the book's unique content.
  - Rotate frequently between: "Two Truths and a Lie", "Prediction Box", "Rhyme Detective", "Best Choice Justification", "What's Missing?", "Choice Combo", and "Memory Challenge".
