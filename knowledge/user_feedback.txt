- STRICT STORY TENSE MATCHING (MANDATORY & NON-NEGOTIABLE):
  - Check the 'original_story_tense' from the Book Analysis.
  - ALL story comprehension questions and answers MUST strictly match the story's tense!
  - IF THE BOOK IS PAST TENSE (e.g. went, looked, ran, saw):
    * ✅ USE: "Where did Hermit Crab go? / She went inside..."
    * ✅ USE: "What did Rico see? / He saw a dog..."
    * ❌ BANNED: "Where is Hermit Crab going?" or "What is Rico seeing?" (DO NOT use present tense for past tense stories!).
  - Present Continuous ("What is [character] doing?") is ONLY allowed for picture observation questions.

- NO DUPLICATE VOCABULARY WORDS IN JSON:
  - In the JSON output, the "meaning" field MUST NOT repeat the "word" or include a dash.
  - ❌ BAD JSON: "word": "classroom", "meaning": "classroom — A special room..."
  - ✅ GOOD JSON: "word": "classroom", "meaning": "A special room with desks and chairs where students learn! What color is your classroom door?"

- INTERACTIVE VOCABULARY FORMAT (BLUE FONT):
  - Vocabulary entries MUST follow this pattern: word — Guiding explanation! Interactive question?
  - Example: "moon — The big round white ball glowing in the night sky! Have you seen the moon at night?"
  - NEVER repeat the word twice. NEVER use letter-counting gimmicks.

- ROTATE GAMES FREQUENTLY:
  - Select 2 DIFFERENT games from the QKids Game Bank for each book!
  - Rotate frequently between: "Sentence Builder", "Memory Challenge", "Category Sort", "Prediction Box", "Best Choice", and "Count & Speak". NO live-typing games.

- ABSOLUTELY BANNED COVER COLOR QUESTIONS:
  - Do NOT ask "What color..." or "What colors do you see..." on the cover page!
  - Cover questions MUST ONLY ask:
    1. "What's the title of the book?"
    2. "Who or what is on the cover?"
    3. "What is happening in the picture?" or "Where are they?"

- QUESTION QUANTITY & RATIO (>= 5 QUESTIONS PER PAGE MANDATORY):
  - Every page MUST contain AT LEAST 5 distinct questions.
  - 80% Content-Based (Visual Observation, Story Plot, Character Feelings directly on Page N).
  - 20% Elaboration / Open-Ended (Personal Connection, Real-World Knowledge).

- CONDITIONAL SCENARIO ELABORATION ("What do you need...?"):
  - Apply "What do you need when..." ONLY when the page depicts a significant scenario (swimming, riding a bike, cooking). Do NOT force it on minor pages.

- GUIDED RETELLING SCAFFOLDING:
  - Retelling lines MUST contain blank underlines ______ AND parenthetical answers at the end: "First, Bert and Beth ______ to the ______ on a sunny day. (went; beach)".
  - NO square brackets [...] in retelling.

- NO "MAKE A SENTENCE" QUESTIONS ON PAGES.
- NO childish role-play, TPR, or physical movement games (~10+ yo students).
- Page N questions must ONLY ask about Page N. No "Looking back at page X" questions.
