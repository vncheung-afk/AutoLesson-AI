- NO DUPLICATE VOCABULARY WORDS IN JSON (STRICT RULE):
  - Do NOT include the vocabulary word or a dash inside the JSON "meaning" field!
  - The "word" field contains the word (e.g. "moon").
  - The "meaning" field contains ONLY the explanation and follow-up question.
  - ❌ BAD JSON: "word": "moon", "meaning": "moon — A big bright ball! Have you seen the moon?"
  - ✅ GOOD JSON: "word": "moon", "meaning": "A big bright ball glowing in the night sky! Have you seen the moon at night?"

- NO QUESTION CATEGORY LABELS OR PREFIXES:
  - Do NOT prefix questions with category labels like "Visual observation:", "Content Q1:", "Story comprehension:", "Elaboration:", or "Personal connection:".
  - Write ONLY the clean, direct question text!
  - ❌ BAD: "Visual observation: What can you see in the picture?"
  - ✅ GOOD: "What can you see in the picture?"

- BANNED COVER COLOR QUESTIONS:
  - Do NOT ask "What color..." or "What colors do you see..." on the cover page!
  - Cover questions MUST ONLY ask:
    1. "What's the title of the book?"
    2. "Who or what is on the cover?"
    3. "What is happening in the picture?" or "Where are they?"

- QUESTION QUANTITY & RATIO (>= 5 QUESTIONS PER PAGE MANDATORY):
  - Every page MUST contain AT LEAST 5 distinct questions.
  - 80% of questions MUST be Content-Based (Visual Observation, Story Plot, Character Feelings directly on Page N).
  - 20% of questions MUST be Elaboration / Open-Ended (Personal Connection, Real-World Knowledge).

- CONDITIONAL SCENARIO ELABORATION ("What do you need...?"):
  - Apply "What do you need when..." ONLY when the page depicts a significant scenario (swimming, riding a bike, cooking). Do NOT force it on minor pages.

- GUIDED RETELLING SCAFFOLDING:
  - Retelling lines MUST contain blank underlines ______ AND parenthetical answers at the end: "First, Bert and Beth ______ to the ______ on a sunny day. (went; beach)".
  - NO square brackets [...] in retelling.

- NO "MAKE A SENTENCE" QUESTIONS ON PAGES.
- NO childish role-play, TPR, or physical movement games (~10+ yo students).
- Story questions must strictly match the original story tense.
- Page N questions must ONLY ask about Page N. No "Looking back at page X" questions.
