0: ESL Picture Book Lesson Plan
1: Book: Kitty Cat and the Frog by Annette Smith, illustrated by Ben Spiby
2: Level: Beginner / Elementary (early narrative reader)
3: Duration: 25 minutes
4: 
5: Teaching Intention Analysis
6: Story Summary: Kitty Cat meets a little frog by the pond. When Fat Cat comes to catch the frog, Kitty Cat distracts him so the frog can get away safely.
7: 
8: Teaching Goals:
9: • Language Goal: Simple past tense storytelling (went, ran, looked, hit, came, said)
10: • Vocabulary Goal: Animal actions and story phrases (ran, hit, went into, came out of, get away)
11: • Thinking Goal: Understanding character motivation and problem-solving (why characters act; predicting outcomes)

137: Part 3: Game & Wrap-up (20:00–25:00)
138: Game 1: What Happened Next?
139: Target language: went, ran, looked, said, hit (simple past)
140: How to play: The teacher reads one sentence from the story (e.g., "Fat Cat ran to the pond.") without showing the picture. The student guesses what happens next using the frame "I think next, ______." Then the teacher reveals the real next event so the student can compare.
141: Student output: Past-tense prediction sentences using story vocabulary.
142: 
143: Game 2: Cat or Frog?
144: Target language: I will ______. / He/She will ______.
145: How to play: The teacher names a character (Kitty Cat, Fat Cat, or the frog). The student speaks one sentence in that character's voice about what they will do next, using "I will ______." Then the student picks a character for the teacher.
146: Student output: First-person sentences using "will" + base verb.

148: Story Retelling
149: First, Kitty Cat went outside because ______. (she liked to sit in the sun)
150: Then, a little frog came out of the grass, and it went into the water because ______. (it was scared of Kitty Cat)
151: Next, Fat Cat ran to the pond because ______. (he wanted to catch the frog)
152: After that, Kitty Cat hit Fat Cat's tail because ______. (she wanted to help the frog)
153: Finally, the little frog could get away because ______. (Kitty Cat stopped Fat Cat)
